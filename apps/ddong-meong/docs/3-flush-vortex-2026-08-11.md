# ddong-meong/3 — catalogue simplification and central flush vortex — 2026-08-11

## Trial

Remove the active temporary `dummy` meditation from the working `/3` product
and make the flush itself carry the comic material transition instead of
holding on a black screen after it has already finished.

## Participant situation

After entering their nickname, the participant can choose any of the five
guided meditations. The card press starts the soundtrack in the same user
gesture before navigating to the reader, so the sound begins at content entry
rather than awaiting an additional interaction. During a session, pressing the
existing flush control immediately stops text motion, sound and new material
events. The already-present lower accumulation field and its reservoir particles
rotate around the existing below-screen drain, contract into it, and disappear
with the end of the 2.8-second flush.

## Changed variable

- The active catalogue and content registry now include only the five guided
  readings. `/ddong-meong/3/dummy` no longer has a generated route; its image
  asset and active reader component are removed. The lyric `readingScript`
  remains only as a compile-time dependency of the unmodified `mobile copy`
  reference and is not reachable from `/3`.
- `/ddong-meong/3/main` restores the existing card list for all five guided
  readings. The original new-tab behavior is removed so the soundtrack element
  started by the card press persists into the reader in the same document.
- The production reader and the background lab now end their cycle at the
  2.8-second drain boundary. The former 5.5-second production black hold and
  the lab's 0.7-second reset hold are removed.
- `uFlushProgress` remains the sole flush clock. The original field-height
  collapse, lower funnel, atmosphere fade and falling-material fade remain the
  checkpoint. Only the existing field sampling coordinates and reservoir
  particle positions now rotate and contract into that same lower drain. No
  full-frame overlay, added spiral image, centre hole, or solid-form remapping
  is used.

## Retained invariants

- The entry flow, card geometry, typography, reader layout, timed text,
  soundtrack handling, flush control, socket events and five guided profile
  definitions remain unchanged.
- The `mobile copy` baseline is not modified.
- The test lab remains a separate full-viewport visual surface; it shares the
  renderer to expose the same flush relation without changing product routing.

## Observation status

The HTTPS background lab was observed after a populated reservoir was flushed.
The liquid boundary still follows the retained downward-drain timing; its
material grain and particles now turn into the lower drain rather than being
replaced by a separate visual layer. A rejected branch advected the
`fieldMask` itself and produced a large crescent ribbon, so it was removed.
There is no added image, full-screen spiral, or replacement geometry in the
accepted branch.
