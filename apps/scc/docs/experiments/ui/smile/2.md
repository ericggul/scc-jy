# UI Smile 2 — N concentric faces

- **Route:** `/ui/smile/2`
- **Date:** 2026-09-04

## Trial contract

1. All `N` faces share the viewport midpoint.
2. The largest radius is half the viewport diagonal, reaching all four corners.
3. The remaining radii descend to one `N`th of that radius at a
   constant linear step.
4. The field is static: no face grows or animates.
5. All faces retain the smiling state. From the largest face to the smallest,
   each successive radius turns its face a further fixed 1° around the shared
   centre.
