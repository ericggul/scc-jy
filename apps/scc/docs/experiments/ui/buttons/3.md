# UI Buttons 3 — MP3 transport field

- **Route:** `/ui/buttons/3`
- **Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Baseline:** the dense Canvas interaction and bottom source selector retained
  as an independent fork of `ui/buttons/2`.

## Trial contract

1. **Participant situation:** a viewer encounters the familiar MP3 transport
   symbols without a player, track, timeline, or device frame.
2. **Primary parameter:** a repeated local cycle of `Play → Pause → Stop`.
3. **Perceptual job:** let the compact circular control remain recognisable
   while its transport states become a page-wide changing pattern.
4. **Interaction job:** mouse entry advances only the circle under the pointer.
   The field begins with a repeating combination of play, pause, and stop
   symbols; no click or sound is implied.
5. **Wrapper justification:** circular transport controls are an operational
   MP3 grammar rather than an audio-themed decoration. Removing the player
   isolates the button's minimal command language.
6. **Removal test:** no track title, progress bar, album art, device bezel, or
   playback status is present. The repeated controls alone carry the trial.

## Black-and-white image trial

- The lower `Play` control restores the abstract transport pattern. The four
  image thumbnails select Mona Lisa, Great Wave, Earthrise, or Eiffel Tower.
- The selected source is sampled against the real square control grid aspect
  ratio, cover-cropped from centre without stretching.
- Luminance is reduced to exactly black or white with Floyd–Steinberg error
  diffusion. Black samples become filled circles with white transport glyphs;
  white samples remain white circles with black outlines and glyphs. The image
  is therefore carried by controls, not placed behind or over them.
- Hover preserves each sampled black/white value and changes only the MP3
  symbol. The image can be disturbed locally without erasing the circular
  button grammar.

## Invariants

- The zero-margin field meets the viewport edges and crops excess circles at
  those edges.
- Every mark is a 14px circular button using only `#000` and `#fff`.
- The visible field is one Canvas 2D surface. A compact `Uint8Array` stores
  the three transport states; mouse movement hit-tests one cell and redraws
  only that circle.
- The source sampler returns one `Uint8Array` of binary tones rather than a
  per-cell object graph. Full redraws occur only after grid or source changes;
  canvas backing resolution is capped at twelve million pixels.
