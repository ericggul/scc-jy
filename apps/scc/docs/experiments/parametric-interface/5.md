# Parametric Interface 5 — Gmail inbox arrival stream

Route: `/parametric-interface/5`

## Trial contract

- **Participant situation:** a desktop Gmail user is scanning a normal inbox
  while new messages accumulate at its top.
- **Primary parameter:** ordinary read email prepends after a random 600–1320
  ms hold. At each audio-timed lyric word, a starred `SIGNALS WE KEEP` arrival
  is added; the word is its uppercase Gmail subject and the complete current
  lyric cue is its uppercase preview.
- **Perceptual job:** follow a heterogeneous stream of work, collaboration,
  orders, newsletters, and suspicious mail, then encounter the lyric as an
  ordinary arriving email rather than a permanent centre-row feature.
- **Interaction job:** Gmail's selection, refresh, pagination, category, and
  mailbox controls retain their familiar positions and affordances; none gains
  a parametric role.
- **Wrapper justification:** Gmail is reproduced as an actual inbox context
  (header, search, mailbox navigation, inbox toolbar, category tabs, email-row
  selection geometry, message density, and Workspace side panel), not as a
  single generic email card.
- **System family:** static inbox mail remains fixed below the arrival stream.
  Every arrival uses Gmail's normal sender, subject, timestamp, selection
  control, and preview slots. The lyric has no special central placement or
  treatment beyond the ordinary filled Gmail star.
- **Removal test:** remove the lyric arrival template and the remaining screen
  is a complete, legible stream of ordinary Gmail inbox mail.

## Preserved invariants

- arriving mail prepends one row at a time above the fixed baseline list; all
  ordinary mail is read, while only `SIGNALS WE KEEP` lyric mail is starred
  and unread;
- lyric email uses the current word in the ordinary Gmail subject slot and the
  complete lyric cue in the ordinary preview slot, not an overlay, label,
  badge, or special styling;
- the desktop reading composition fits within one viewport and adapts by
  collapsing Gmail's secondary navigation at narrower widths;
- reduced motion suppresses nonessential arrival animation without changing
  the audio lyric clock.
