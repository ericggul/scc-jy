## Bounded relation

mobile home view: header, story rail, visible post, and floating primary navigation.
The device status bar is deliberately excluded. This is a local reconstruction,
not an archived replay of Instagram's private application.

## Invariants

- No white wrapper, arbitrary border, device status-bar imitation, `LIVE` badge,
  or centred mock-phone is added.
- Story buttons open a local viewer state; the post toggles a like response; header
  controls open/close their local sheet; and primary navigation updates its selected
  state. Those are local state paths, not claims to replay Instagram's private data.
- This is a fixed evidenced initial state, not a claim to reproduce other account,
