# SNS Instagram 3 — socially propagated story states

- **Route:** `/sns/instagram/3`
- **Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Baseline:** `/sns/instagram/2`

## Bounded relation

The fixed story-bubble grid from the preceding trial is retained while its
independent per-cell loop is replaced by a local social-propagation model.
An empty cell can receive a new story from a currently new neighbouring cell
through weighted local ties plus one short-range social tie. The responsible
source and target are briefly revealed by a fading, directional trace.

## Invariants

- Bubble geometry, centered responsive grid, display controls, and non-clickable
  story cells remain the same as `instagram/2`.
- A story state progresses from empty to new, then through a directional viewing
  sweep and a brief same-position leaving fade before returning to empty.
- Viewing uses an exponentially sampled waiting time, the inter-arrival law of
  a Poisson process. There is no lingering viewed state after the sweep.

## Changed variable

- New-story appearance is no longer an independent local clock. It combines a
  low spontaneous arrival rate with probabilistic exposure from active incoming
  ties. Newly influenced arrivals retain a short-lived directed origin-to-target
  trace rather than a persistent PageRank-style arrow.

## Unresolved question

Whether the spatially local ties should stay visible only at the instant of
propagation, or accumulate into a longer-lived social topology, remains for a
separate fork.
