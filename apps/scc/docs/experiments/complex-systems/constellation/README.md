# Constellation experiment

Route: `/constellation/1`, registered through the constellation experiment
registry and owned by the filesystem-only `complex-systems` group.

Date: 2026-08-14.

## Constellation/1 — resource-mediated coevolution

1. **Participant situation:** one person observes a finite spatial population and
   may introduce a bounded local resource pulse by pressing the field.
2. **Primary parameter:** the reciprocal dependence between individual energy
   and relation strength: energy differences produce flow, flow reinforces a
   relation, and relation maintenance changes both endpoints' survival budget.
3. **Perceptual job:** distinguish energy-funded birth, starvation or age death,
   locally probable relation formation, and unused-relation decay without a
   scheduled global sequence selecting those events.
4. **Interaction job:** supply one locality and anticipate an indirect response,
   or adjust the five causal rates in the lower strip and compare the resulting
   population and topology. The strip changes the running model immediately.
5. **Wrapper justification:** a single uninterrupted dark field makes changing
   luminosity and weak relation loss perceptible. The darkness is a contrast
   condition for model state, not inherited complex-systems styling or generic
   futuristic atmosphere. Resource density appears only as spatially smoothed
   blue light derived from the finite model grid.
6. **System family:** only the client-side seeded model, stable model-layer
   identifiers, direct local intervention, and one-viewport observation remain.
   No visual grammar is inherited from another complex-systems route.
7. **Removal test:** stars, relation strength, resource density, actual flow,
   local supply, and the explicitly requested lower parameter strip remain. The
   strip contains only resource return, energy cost, birth threshold, relation
   formation, relation decline, an energy-size visualization toggle, pause, and
   restart. Energy-size is enabled by default: each stable star ID retains a
   short-lived presentation radius that approaches the radius derived from its
   current energy, making energy gain and loss visible as expansion and
   contraction. Disabling it returns mature stars to one common radius. Radius
   memory remains client presentation state and never enters the causal model.
   The prior oversized serif
   title, corner instructions, metric block, topology counter, visible buttons,
   pale paper field, and monospaced interface remain removed because they
   competed with the constellation and made the route resemble a generated
   dashboard template. Parameter controls form one continuous lower zone rather
   than cards or elements scattered over the sky.

## Bounded trial

- **Baseline:** no earlier local algorithm is treated as a scientific baseline;
  the trial starts from the adaptive-network requirement that node dynamics and
  topology feed back into one another.
- **Changed variable:** vertex birth/death and edge formation/severance all draw
  from the same resource and energy economy. They are not four independent
  visual event clocks.
- **Resource rule:** a finite spatial grid diffuses and regenerates slowly.
  Individuals consume their local cell. Metabolism and relation maintenance
  dissipate energy, with a fraction returned to the field. A participant pulse
  adds a finite amount to nearby cells.
- **Birth/death rule:** reproduction requires high individual energy, sufficient
  local resource, and an explicit energy cost paid by the parent. Offspring
  inherit a locally mutated interaction trait. Starvation and age raise the
  individual death hazard; residual energy is partially returned locally.
- **Relation rule:** nearby pairs form relations through a stochastic local
  hazard combining distance, trait compatibility, degree cost, and energy
  difference. A relation conducts energy from the richer endpoint to the poorer
  endpoint with loss. Measured flow reinforces its strength; maintenance and
  inactivity weaken it until severance.
- **Spatial rule:** positions are model state, not a force-layout rendering.
  Individuals respond to the resource gradient, short-range crowding, relation
  tension, weak local noise, and reflecting boundaries.
- **Stochastic boundary:** a seeded generator supplies local variation and
  event hazards. Randomness perturbs state-dependent probabilities; it does not
  independently select a global birth, death, formation, or severance phase.
  Every time step also applies a seeded random permutation to asynchronous
  micro-updates so stable array or identifier order does not grant persistent
  priority in resource competition, relation formation, reproduction, or death.
- **Observable result:** pure model tests verify seeded determinism, stable and
  unique identities, valid endpoints, finite non-negative state, finite local
  intervention, and the occurrence of all four topology operations over a
  four-minute simulated observation.
- **Unresolved question:** the regenerating resource makes this an open
  dissipative system. A later trial could compare it with a strictly conserved
  material budget and measure whether its apparent metastability survives.
