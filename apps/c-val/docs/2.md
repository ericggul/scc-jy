## Today's exact task

C-VAL 2 must let an ordinary person discover a repeatable relation between a
directional phone movement and the market price:

```text
move the phone this way -> price rises
move it the opposite way -> price falls
move it around a balancing direction -> price stays relatively still
```

V/A/L are necessary intermediate market conditions. They are not GUI controls,
three modes to select, or target numbers the participant must precisely learn.
The participant learns the bodily `phone movement -> price` relation by trying
directions and watching the actual market response.

Two requirements have exactly equal priority. Neither may be demoted to an
edge case:

1. Every direction and the full range of ways a person may rotate, tilt, turn,
   or shake the phone must continue to affect the system.
2. The small movements made by most people holding a phone normally must
   already cause large, immediate and enjoyable executed-price variation.

Passing only one requirement is failure. Full-direction mathematical coverage
with weak ordinary movement is failure; strong small movement on one privileged
axis while other directions disappear is also failure.

The complete active chain is:

```text
one human phone movement
  -> one simple, fixed sensor-to-V/A/L relation
  -> V/A/L change together
  -> fixed V/A/L market direction and strength
  -> ordinary market agents change order sides and valuations
  -> FIFO executions
  -> actual displayed price
  -> surrounding social screens
```

There is no second input channel and no direct assignment of displayed price.
Price has no configured ceiling. Its only bound is a one-tick positive floor
of `0.01`, because a traded equity price cannot cross below zero.

## Non-negotiable interaction rules

1. The only participant input is moving, shaking, or rotating the phone.
2. In the final participant interaction, after browser-required motion
   permission, there is no V/A/L selector,
   slider, mode button, gesture tutorial, BUY/SELL control, or commit action.
   The current mapping buttons are temporary author validation controls: they
   operate before permission, reset the shared market, and never set V/A/L.
3. The relation remains fixed and does not learn hidden axes or modes from the
   participant.
4. Pitch, roll, twist, inversion, continuous turns and ordinary small wrist
   movement all remain materially effective under the same rule.
5. V/A/L immediately alter one existing agent-and-order-book market path.
6. No `pressure`, privileged operator, hidden gesture classifier, RMS window,
   cadence detector, dwell gate, or pose-hold condition exists.
7. The mobile screen gives feedback; it does not become a second control
   surface.
8. Do not add separate small-angle logic, large-turn logic, inversion logic,
   axis fallbacks, per-case exceptions, or a hierarchy of ordinary versus
   exceptional users. If the mapping needs a growing case list, reject it.
9. Internal mathematical sophistication is not a success criterion. Use the
   smallest relation that produces the required human experience.

## Current lifecycle retained independently of the rejected mapping

Before meaningful movement:

- phase is `waiting`;
- price is exactly `100`;
- there are no participants, resting orders, executions, turnover, or depth;
- the random market process does not advance.

The live client reads `DeviceMotionEvent.rotationRate`. A total rotation energy
of `1 degree/second` is the only sensor-noise boundary. Crossing it activates
the market once. Reset from the controller returns the complete system to the
dormant state; no neutral pose or coordinate frame is learned.

After activation the market continues running. When the phone stops rotating
or current input disappears, V/A/L return toward neutral and the
directional bias diminishes rather than inventing a new gesture state.

## Current WIP phone-to-V/A/L mapping

The current trial uses the browser's three rotation-rate components directly,
in degrees/second:

```text
energy = |alpha| + |beta| + |gamma|
signed = alpha + beta + gamma

intensity = energy / (12 + energy)
direction = signed / (12 + |signed|)

V = 0.50 + 0.48 * intensity
A = 0.50 + 0.48 * direction
L = 0.50 - 0.48 * intensity
```

This is one equation for every axis and magnitude. It has no absolute pose,
quaternion, baseline, learned axis, temporal window, dominant-axis choice,
inversion branch or special small-angle mode. Ordinary motion is deliberately
strong: 10 degrees/second on any one axis gives approximately 72/72/28, and 20
degrees/second gives approximately 80/80/20. Reversing the same motion preserves
V/L intensity and reverses A. Values approach but never reach exact 0 or 100.

This mapping is not yet accepted. Its exact post-checkpoint context, prior
failures, retained invariants and real-phone acceptance questions are recorded
in [`2-iteration-ledger-2026-08-05.md`](./2-iteration-ledger-2026-08-05.md).

## Rejected motion-path phone-to-V/A/L mapping

The following is the rejected implementation, retained only as a failure
record. The prior beta-only baseline is preserved in
[`2-working-baseline-2026-08-04.md`](./2-working-baseline-2026-08-04.md). It was
replaced because normal turns remained hard-clamped at 0/100 outside ±35
degrees and ignored twist, inversion and complete rotations.

Each raw sample is converted using the W3C Z-X'-Y'' quaternion construction.
The live tracker computes:

```text
qStep = inverse(qPrevious) * qCurrent

deviceAngularVelocity = shortest-axis-angle(qStep) / elapsed time
angularVelocity       = rotate into the initial phone frame
```

Quaternion sign continuity prevents alpha/beta/gamma wraps from becoming false
human reversals. The angular-velocity vector preserves the path of a 180- or
360-degree turn while it is happening, even when the final pose returns to its
start. Absolute distance from the initial pose is deliberately excluded: the
failed preceding trial proved that it pins V high and L low after a flip.

At each sample, the largest signed component of the 3D angular-velocity vector
sets direction. This lets pitch, roll or twist lead without diluting it through
the other two axes. Speed, direction and path coherence are separate signals:

```text
speed     = length(angularVelocity)
direction = dominant signed component(angularVelocity)
coherence = cosine(current velocity, previous velocity)

V = 0.50 + 0.44 * soft((speed - 4.0) / 3.5)
A = 0.50 + 0.44 * soft(direction / 5.0)
L = 0.50 + 0.44 * coherence
```

Here `soft(x) = x / sqrt(1 + x²)`. The live target stays between 6 and 94;
exact 0/100 values are unreachable. Continued direction gives high L, reversal
gives low L, and irregular cross-axis motion crosses the interior. When speed
falls below the noise boundary, the live target is exactly 50/50/50. V/A/L are
three observations of one physical path, not selectable controls.

## Fixed V/A/L-to-market mapping

V, A, and L keep their ordinary market roles:

- V controls information and valuation dispersion and quote risk;
- A controls the rate of agent orders;
- L controls passive supply, replenishment, spread, depth, and price impact.

For the bodily direction to be learnable, the same intermediate values also
produce one fixed signed condition direction:

```text
signed activity = 2 * (A - 0.5)
volatility gain = 0.25 + 0.75 * V
liquidity gain  = 1 - 0.75 * L
market direction = signed activity * volatility gain * liquidity gain
```

This is not an additional participant parameter. It is a deterministic market
consequence of the current V/A/L state:

- A above `50` biases valuation changes and ordinary agent order sides upward;
- A below `50` biases them downward with symmetric strength;
- A at `50` cancels toward stillness;
- higher V strengthens either direction;
- higher L damps either direction and lower L exposes larger price impact.

Strong direction suppresses unrelated random innovation, so opposite repeated
phone directions remain perceptually opposite instead of being hidden by
noise. All price changes still come from real FIFO executions by the existing
participant types. There is no privileged human order and no direct mutation of
the displayed price.

## What the participant should learn

The participant should not need to name alpha, beta, gamma, V, A, or L. Through
repetition they should form an embodied expectation:

- one region/direction of motion makes executed price rise;
- its opposite makes executed price fall;
- the neutral pose and stillness produce little directional drive;
- continuing a turn through inversion or 360 degrees remains observable while
  it happens;
- motion that raises liquidity makes the same drive less destructive;
- motion that lowers liquidity makes it more destructive.

The mapping may initially be abstract. It must nevertheless be fixed,
repeatable, immediate, and learnable by a human through action and consequence.

## Mobile feedback screen

The mobile temporarily exposes three compact mapping-selection buttons for
author comparison. They do not set V/A/L or alter the market model. Selecting
one stops sensor listening, resets the same C-VAL 2 market to 100, and requires
`ENABLE MOTION` again. Remove this selector after comparative validation.
The exact sensor, baseline, engagement, equation and range specification for
all three choices—and the template for recording their pros and cons—is in the
[`post-checkpoint ledger`](./2-iteration-ledger-2026-08-05.md#exact-specifications-of-the-three-currently-selectable-mappings).

- one horizontal line mirrors the current combined signed direction;
- the bottom row exposes current V/A/L intermediate values;
- the center shows the actual last executed price;
- `RISING`, `FALLING`, `STILL`, or `WAITING` comes from the actual one-second
  execution window, not from predicted direction.

Apart from that temporary test selector, this closes the learning loop on the same device: bodily motion, intermediate
conditions, and realized price are visible together. The screen never asks the
person to tap V/A/L or choose how the movement should be interpreted.

## Multiple humans

During normal use, every phone must use the same selected phone-to-V/A/L
equation. Current contributions use one transparent rule: an equal-weight
arithmetic mean separately for V, A, and L. Comparative validation should use
one active phone; otherwise different button selections would be averaged and
would no longer constitute a controlled comparison.
The V/A/L-to-market equation is then applied once to that shared state. There
is no special participant priority and no opposing hidden pressure channel.

### Media multiplicity trial — 2026-08-11

The media screen uses one repeated tile per absolute percentage-point move and
a 400-tile ceiling. Both values are configured in
`components/c-val/2/screen/media/config.ts`. A non-zero fractional move still
shows one tile; subsequent counts use the ceiling of the absolute percentage
change. The two direction-selected source segments, row-major square grid,
full-bleed composition, and single active video decoder remain invariant.
Rendering is scheduled from decoded video frames when the browser supports that
callback, with animation-frame fallback, and the reusable tile canvas is resized
only when its cell dimensions change. Static tests verify the 1:1 mapping and
400-cell / 20-by-20 bound. Smoothness at the ceiling remains a target-device
browser measurement rather than a static performance guarantee.

## Idle closing auction and simultaneous phones — 2026-08-18

The active-phone aggregation now adds each current phone's displacement from
neutral V/A/L instead of averaging packets or selecting the most recent phone.
If every contribution becomes stale or explicitly disengages, the runtime holds
for 20 seconds, then stops in `closing-auction` at its last execution-derived
state. No price is interpolated to `100`, and the order book, market history,

## Verification truth

Static tests for the current WIP establish that:

- waiting state remains exactly empty at `100`;
- ordinary 10–20 degrees/second motion already causes a strong V/A/L condition;
- alpha, beta and gamma use the exact same mapping;
- reversing any axis reverses A without weakening V/L;
- mixed opposing axes retain motion energy even when signed direction cancels;
- large rates approach but never reach exact 0/100 endpoints;
- opposite V/A/L states have opposite executed-price directions
  across multiple deterministic market paths;
- neutral pose is balanced and L damps rather than reverses direction;
- all prices remain execution-derived and the market conserves cash/inventory;
- V, A, and L retain distinct volatility, activity, and liquidity effects.
- outbound Discord, Slack, and Telegram tests prove that disabled transports
  make no network request and rate-limited transports retain only latest state.
  Slack's and Telegram's sustained one-second per-channel/per-chat floors stay
  separate from the faster Discord projection.

The synthetic orientation shake robustness suite remains a market-engine test;
it does not represent the live `rotationRate` sensor path and cannot substitute
for real-phone judgment. Static tests verify equations, market integrity,
magnitude and sign only. They do not satisfy the human discoverability gate.

Static tests cannot establish whether a real person can discover the bodily
mapping quickly or whether its timing and magnitude feel right. That must be
judged on a real phone against the controller. The rejected implementations are
recorded separately in
[`2-interaction-failure-review.md`](./2-interaction-failure-review.md).
